Common questions

Let's clear a few things up.

Do I need analytics before starting?

No. An initial measurement plan can begin with clarifying the actions and outcomes your business cares about.

Can strategy be a standalone service?

Yes. Some teams need a roadmap or audit before committing to ongoing campaigns.

Does analytics require a large tech stack?

Not necessarily. Simple, reliable measurement is more useful than many disconnected dashboards.
